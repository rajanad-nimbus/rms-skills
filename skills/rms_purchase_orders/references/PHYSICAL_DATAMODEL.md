## Table: ORDER_TYPES

**Description:** This table holds valid WMS order types. The order type value will be passed to the WMS to inform the warehouse of the distribution method to use. Valid order types are AUTOMATIC, MANUAL, WAVE, PO, and PREDIST. AUTOMATIC, WAVE and MANUAL are used for transfers and standalone allocations. PO and PREDIST are used for allocations tied to a purchase order.
